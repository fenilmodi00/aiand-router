Is full Fireworks–Pioneer parity achievable?

Yes, with compute — not yet achieved. Competitors do not require a ~1TB local image farm; official SWE-bench cloud is Modal / sb-cli (SWE-bench FAQ). Pioneer’s documented policy (complexity → calibrated P(success) → cheapest above threshold/max_regret) already matches our scorer shape (Pioneer router). FireRouter is a different product shape: binary redirect vs pass-through (FireRouter overview). Our cascade seam is a dormant prototype (0 redirects at ship knobs) — not FireRouter parity.
